Help

Install it. Start a song.

LyricTile runs independently on an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 27 or later. Nearpane is not required.

Installation

The first public release is being prepared. Check the product page for its actual status. Engineering candidates and old notarized build 4 are not the latest final package.

  1. Once the final installer is available, download the DMG from the product page and drag LyricTile into Applications.
  2. Launch the copy in Applications, then eject the installer disk.
  3. Read the welcome screen. Online lyrics lookup starts enabled and can be turned off there or in settings.
  4. Play a song in a supported player. Allow macOS Automation access to that player when requested.

Everyday use

The panel appears while a song plays. The current line is centered, and long lines adapt to the available space. Hover for song details, playback controls, and progress. Move away to return to lyrics. The panel hides when playback stops.

When lyrics are unavailable, you see a compact song summary. Use the song menu to choose another lyric version, import a local file, or adjust timing. Supported formats include LRC, TTML, Lyricsfile, and plain text. Plain text without time tags does not synchronize automatically.

No panel appears

Confirm that the player is actually playing a song, then check the source shown in the menu bar. Hiding while paused is expected. If Apple Music or Spotify Automation access was denied, retry through the menu-bar guidance or change it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

Song details appear without lyrics

Check online lookup and your connection. Live, cover, and remastered versions may not have a matching lyric file. Choose another version or import your own. A player that does not supply a usable position also cannot support reliable automatic synchronization.

Updates and your files

Use the app's dedicated update entry once a final update is available, or quit the app and replace it from a newer DMG. You do not need to erase preferences or saved lyrics. Clearing the automatic cache preserves imported and explicitly bound lyrics and their timing adjustments.

Player support

Local-track playback and core controls have been verified with Apple Music 1.7, QQ Music 11.11.1 and NetEase Music 3.1.14. Spotify remains unverified, and commercial streaming catalogs have not been qualified as a whole. When a player does not provide a reliable position, progress is temporarily unavailable; synchronization resumes with the real playback clock. Exact player versions, source commits, and current results are in the acceptance record. Lyrics coverage and word-level time tags vary by song.
