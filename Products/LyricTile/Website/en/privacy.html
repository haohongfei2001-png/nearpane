Privacy

LyricTile is an independent macOS lyrics companion by HOLDSNOW. No account is needed. The app has no advertising, telemetry, or service that uploads listening history to HOLDSNOW.

Player information

The app reads the song title, artist, album, duration, playback position/state, and artwork supplied by your player to display lyrics and operate playback controls. macOS requests Automation access to Apple Music or Spotify when needed. Normal lyrics features do not need Microphone, Screen Recording, Accessibility, or Full Disk Access.

Online lyrics

Lookup starts enabled, is explained on the welcome screen, and can be disabled in settings. While enabled, the current song's title, artist, album, and duration are sent to LRCLIB. The service also receives the IP address needed for the connection and an application User-Agent. LyricTile does not send audio, imported lyric files, account credentials, or a complete listening history. Providers handle requests under their own terms. Turning lookup off stops new requests; saved lyrics remain available.

Stored on your Mac

Lyrics and manual bindings are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/HOLDSNOW/LyricTile/; preferences use macOS application preferences. The automatic cache is limited to 64 MiB, 256 entries, and a seven-day age limit. Imported or explicitly bound files are protected from automatic eviction. Clear Cache preserves those files and timing adjustments. Uninstalling the app does not automatically erase saved lyrics.

Artwork, updates, and diagnostics

A player may provide local artwork; the Spotify adapter may fetch an artwork URL supplied by the player. Update checks contact the configured GitHub HTTPS feed and download address. Automatic checks are initially off. Hosting providers receive ordinary connection information. The app operates no separate analytics service.

Explicitly exported diagnostics may contain song titles, player versions, playback times, system information, and local paths. They remain at your chosen location and are not uploaded automatically. Review them before sharing.

Content rights

Lyrics, recordings, and artwork belong to their respective rights holders. Open access to LRCLIB's API does not establish copyright authorization for every lyric. LyricTile includes no third-party lyric catalog and claims no ownership or sublicense to those works. You can inspect lyric sources and import lyrics you are entitled to use.

Contact and this website

Submit privacy questions through GitHub issues. Do not post private records, credentials, or unreviewed diagnostic archives. This product page has no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies; website and download hosts receive ordinary network requests.
