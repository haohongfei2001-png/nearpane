# HOLDSNOW product film language

The product earns attention by staying useful beside the user's work. A real
song, its released artwork and a real foreground-window change carry this film.
Nearpane is named from the opening and owns the final call to action.

## Image and typography

| Element | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Paper | `#F6F6F2` |
| Ink | `#222D2D` |
| Secondary type | `#5C6A69` |
| Latin brand type | Inter Variable, regular/medium, optical size 24 |
| Chinese editorial type | Noto Sans CJK SC Regular |
| Environment | Quiet original coastal photograph, pale sky, dark water |
| Native UI | Recorded macOS pixels, unchanged labels and real pointer |

The generated environment is background material. Product panels, track art,
lyric changes and controls are never generated or redrawn. The album's warm
wood color provides a small natural contrast to the coastal palette.

## Desktop composition

Capture at 3840×2160 on a 1920×1080 logical display. The lyric panel is about
280 logical pixels wide, 28 from the right and 52 from the top. The foreground
app occupies x=120, y=112, width=1352, height=838. This keeps both the work and
the companion in a believable desktop relationship.

Two-module photography uses equal exterior dimensions and matching outer
popover material, with the recorded 16-point gap. The Riftlet interior remains its actual
UI. This exterior harmonization is a user-authorized filming adaptation, not a
claim that the shipping product already has this exact appearance.

## Editing and sound

Keep native interaction at its real speed. Use cuts when the foreground task
or music client changes. Take a detail crop only for an action that benefits
from it; preserve the crop's aspect ratio. Do not reveal unshipped modules in
the management window. Record real drag order changes through the actual
management view; do not substitute desktop drag behavior.

Maintain one audible performance across music-client shots by matching each
source capture to the original recording. This is editorial continuity across
separate recordings, not a product claim about gapless client handoff. A real
pause or seek would require a corresponding sound edit. No voice-over or
synthetic singing is used.

## Mobile adaptation

Compose at 1080×1920. Use the same real app and card crops, with the card at
the upper right, readable native lyrics, and the work window below. Scale each
crop proportionally. This is an editorial layout for a mobile audience, not a
portrait macOS mode or a phone version of Nearpane.

## Brand ending

Nearpane is the primary name. HOLDSNOW appears quietly above it. Use the paired
lines “让音乐，留在手边。” / “Keep the music close.” The verified destination is
`https://nearpane.com/`. Until a matching public release exists, retain the
product-preview label and website invitation. Do not substitute a fabricated
download URL, separate LyricTile CTA, or claim that first-launch defaults and
module downloads are already shipped.

Keep the artist/album artwork credit in-frame and attach the complete source,
license and edit credit in the publication description.
