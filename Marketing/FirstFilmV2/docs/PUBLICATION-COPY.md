## 中文

**Nearpane｜让音乐，留在手边。**

播放一首歌，继续读手边的文件。歌词留在桌面右上角，随音乐变化；鼠标移入，播放控制自然出现。

片中实拍 Apple Music 与 QQ 音乐，并展示与 Riftlet 截图助手的组合。

产品预览。了解 Nearpane：<https://nearpane.com/>

## English

**Nearpane — Keep the music close.**

Play a song. Get back to what you were reading. The lyrics stay close, and playback controls appear when you move in.

Featuring real Apple Music and QQ Music playback, with Riftlet alongside for screenshots.

Product preview. Explore Nearpane: <https://nearpane.com/>
